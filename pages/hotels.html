KHATEGAON LOCAL GUIDE

Hotels & Stays in Khategaon

Khategaon offers a mix of accommodation options catering to highway travelers, families, and budget-conscious visitors.

🏨 Accommodations Resorts & Lodges
PREMIUM & RESORT STAYS

Premium & Full-Service Stays

Resorts and higher-rated properties featuring dining, pool, and modern room amenities.

🏊
Resort & Spa

Hotel HR Dreams & Resort

  • Situated conveniently along National Highway 47 featuring resort-style amenities including an outdoor swimming pool, spa, and hot tub.
  • Guests frequently praise the modern interiors and comfortable bedding inside the well-designed rooms.
  • Travelers highlight excellent highway connectivity and appreciate the quality on-site breakfast.
Approx. ₹3,700 – ₹3,970 NH-47, Sandalpur / Industrial Area
🏨
Rooftop Restaurant & Stay

Hotel Shri Vrinda

  • Tucked away in Rajat Kunj Colony off Nemawar Road, praised for its peaceful environment away from heavy crowds.
  • Standout feature according to visitors is the beautiful rooftop restaurant serving travelers between Indore and Harda.
  • Offers spacious, luxurious rooms with exceptionally polite and helpful hospitality staff.
Approx. ₹2,260 – ₹2,430 Rajat Kunj Colony, Off Nemawar Road
BUDGET & TRANSIT LODGES

Standard & Budget Accommodations

Economical lodgings suited for overnight road stops, family visits, and quick stays.

🏢
Budget Friendly

Hotel Dhruvnanda Palace

  • Located along Nemawar Road, striking a great balance between pricing and utility.
  • Routinely recommended as a highly budget-friendly choice delivering solid value for money.
  • Families note that it feels secure and safe, making it reliable for overnight stays or hosting small gatherings.
Approx. ₹970 – ₹990 Nemawar Road, Khategaon
🛏️
Economy Transit Stay

Palash Hotel

  • A simple, no-frills option right in town catering well to travelers searching for a fast, straightforward night stay.
  • Visitors appreciate the polite behavior of property owners and find room service to be quite fast.
  • Suitable for quick, economical stopovers.
Approx. ₹720 – ₹760 Central Town Location
🏬
Basic Highway Lodge

Hotel Osho Palace

  • Positioned right opposite the Government College along the highway, serving as a basic transit lodge.
  • Provides essential shelter for short roadside rests during continuous long-distance highway travel.

Booking & Reservation Tips

During peak festive periods (such as Navratri at Vijasan Dham or regional wedding seasons), advance online or phone bookings are recommended for stays along Nemawar Road and NH-47.
