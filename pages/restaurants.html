Highway Breakfast Spot

Jaiswal Chat House

  • Perched right on the main highway, serving as a designated breakfast rest stop for commuters.
  • Regular travelers frequently stop specifically for their hot poha and samosas.
  • Offers delicious quick bites like kachori and khamman.

Highway Dhaba

Jain Bhojnalaya Restaurant & Dhaba

  • Located on Indore Road, catering closely to vegetarian families and student groups.

Dining Tips for Visitors

Most highway snack outlets and chat spots open early in the morning for breakfast (starting around 6:30 AM for hot poha and samosa). Sit-down bhojnalayas and family restaurants typically serve lunch from 12:00 PM to 3:30 PM and dinner from 7:00 PM onwards.
